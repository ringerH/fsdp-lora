### Instruction:
Explain the difference between supervised and unsupervised learning.

### Response:
Supervised learning uses labeled data, while unsupervised learning finds patterns without labels.

### Instruction:
Write a short definition of overfitting.

### Response:
Overfitting occurs when a model learns noise in the training data and performs poorly on unseen data.

### Instruction:
Give one application of reinforcement learning.

### Response:
Reinforcement learning is used in training game-playing agents like AlphaGo.
